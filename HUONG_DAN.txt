SENTINELJAR 4.0 - HUONG DAN CHAY

1. Giai nen thu muc jarscanner ra mot cho (vi du C:\jarscanner).
2. Mo file chay_server.bat bang Notepad, doi ADMIN_PASS thanh mat khau cua ban, luu lai.
3. Bam dup chay_server.bat. Cho den khi thay dong "Uvicorn running on http://127.0.0.1:8000".
4. Mo trinh duyet vao http://127.0.0.1:8000 va dang nhap bang tai khoan admin.
5. Vao tab Admin de tao key va tai nhac len.

Chua cau hinh SMTP: ma OTP khi dang ky se hien trong cua so den (CMD).
Chua cau hinh Turnstile: cong xac minh Cloudflare tu tat.
Neu lan truoc chay server cu, hay tat het cua so CMD cu truoc (hoac taskkill /F /IM python.exe).
Khong gui ADMIN_PASS, SMTP_PASS, TURNSTILE_SECRET cho ai.
